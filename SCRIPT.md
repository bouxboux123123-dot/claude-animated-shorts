# How Money Is Printed (The Counterfeit Arms Race)

## Research & Physical Facts
- **Fact 1:** US Currency is printed by the Bureau of Engraving and Printing (BEP) using Intaglio presses. Steel plates apply up to 20 tons of pressure per square inch, creating raised ridges that can be felt by human fingertips.
- **Fact 2:** US currency paper is 75% cotton and 25% linen (manufactured by Crane & Co. since 1879), with embedded red and blue synthetic fibers. It withstands ~4,000 double folds before tearing (compared to ~400 folds for wood pulp paper).
- **Fact 3:** Color-Shifting Ink (OVI - Optically Variable Ink) used on $10, $20, $50, and $100 bills shifts from copper to green when tilted. It contains microscopic multilayer interference flakes oriented with magnetic fields.
- **Fact 4:** The EURion Constellation (Omron rings) is a geometric pattern of five small yellow/green rings embedded in banknote designs worldwide. Xerox, Canon, HP photocopiers and scanner firmware contain machine-vision detection that aborts copying upon recognizing this pattern.
- **Fact 5:** Bureau of Engraving and Printing inspection systems (Large Examining and Printing Equipment - LEPE) photograph 10,000 sheets per hour with computer vision. Defective sheets are automatically laser-indexed and pulverized into shredded briquettes.
